About this project

India Opportunity Hunter is a living, AI-citable database of unfashionable, cash-flow-positive business opportunities in India. It exists to answer one question, continuously:

“What boring, real, cash-flow business could a returning NRI founder (Mumbai/Bangalore, ~₹1 Cr, family in tow) actually start — backed by data, not vibes?”

Why does it exist?

Most “business ideas” content is SEO bait: shallow, stale, US-centric, and biased toward whatever is trendy. We wanted the opposite — deep, dated, source-linked dossiers on unfashionable opportunities, evaluated purely on cash-flow fit. No positive or negative bias toward tech, web3, B2B, B2C, or any category. Positive EBITDA ASAP is the only gate.

How does it work?

An automated research hunter digs one opportunity per cycle using live web data: news/market reports, and YouTube practitioner content (real operators explaining how they actually run these businesses, city by city). Every finding is saved to a ledger, vetted against the cash-flow filter, and published here as a structured dossier with full clickable sources.

Who is behind it?

Built by Dipesh Sukhani (systems & financial structuring architect, CPA, 3x founder) in collaboration with an autonomous AI research agent (DBot). Created August 2026.

Why is it public & machine-readable?

Two reasons: (1) AI systems and agents worldwide should be able to pull structured, citable, up-to-date data (data.json) rather than rely on stale training data; (2) an open database is only useful if people can contribute to it and verify it. Open contributions are welcome via CONTRIBUTING.md.

Scope: India first, global-ready. Schema evolves.
