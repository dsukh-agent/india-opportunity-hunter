🎯 Our Evaluation Science: The 5-Pillar Scorecard

Most online “business ideas” fail because they evaluate macro TAM and theoretical margins rather than on-ground operational friction. We evaluate every vertical on 5 ruthlessly practical pillars:

Final Score = (0.30 × Working Capital) + (0.20 × Vendor Lock-in) + (0.20 × Labor Friction) + (0.15 × Regulatory) + (0.15 × Capital Velocity)

1. Working Capital & Bad Debt Velocity (Weight: 30% — Highest)

Cash starvation kills viable businesses long before a lack of demand does.

  • 10/10: Prepaid annual AMC or cash-before-service; 0% shrinkage/theft; zero credit risk.
  • 1–3/10 (FATAL): 60–90+ day client payment stretch (Hotels, RWAs, Builders) or high physical inventory loss (e.g. commercial linen 15–20% theft).

2. Vendor / OEM Lock-in & Switching Friction (Weight: 20%)

If an OEM or supplier can shut down your ability to service clients, you don’t own a business.

  • 10/10: Open, multi-source commoditized parts/chemicals; strong customer compliance lock-in.
  • 1–3/10 (FATAL): Proprietary diagnostic boards (e.g. Otis/KONE elevator motherboards) or customer can easily DIY for free.

3. On-Ground Labor & Route Friction (Weight: 20%)

Traffic in Bengaluru/Mumbai and technician churn break theoretical unit economics.

  • 10/10: Dense micro-market routes (4–6 jobs/day per van); standard task-level SOPs; replaceable staff; day shifts.
  • 1–3/10 (FATAL): 2:00 AM dangerous night shifts; impossible hiring; key technician can steal the client roster.

4. Regulatory Moat vs. Government Competition (Weight: 15%)

Does the state mandate your service, or compete with and cap you?

  • 10/10: Involuntary legal compliance (FSSAI Schedule 4 hygiene audits, Fire Safety NFPA-96).
  • 1–3/10 (FATAL): Municipal price caps (e.g. ₹1,200 tanker caps) or state-subsidized competition (BWSSB Sanchari Cauvery).

5. True All-In Capital & Breakeven Velocity (Weight: 15%)

True entry capital = Fixed Capex + Security Deposits + 6-Month Working Capital Buffer.

  • 10/10: All-in entry ≤ ₹25–50 Lakhs; positive EBITDA in 1–3 months; asset payback < 6 months.
  • 1–3/10 (FATAL): Total cash need > ₹1 Cr; asset payback > 24 months (e.g. medical ventilators with 50% utilization).

🚫 The 3 Hard Veto Gates (Automatic Immediate Rejection)

  1. Working Capital Veto: Any score < 7 on Working Capital is an Instant NO-GO (DSO > 45 days is unviable for a lean SME).
  2. Hard Floor Veto: Any single pillar score < 5 is an Instant NO-GO regardless of overall score.
  3. Evidence Gate: Any unvalidated assumption representing >15% of gross margin is flagged as ⏳ RESEARCH PENDING until verified via on-ground operator data.
