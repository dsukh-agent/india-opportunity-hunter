About this project

India Opportunity Hunter is a living, AI-citable database of unfashionable, cash-flow-positive business opportunities in India. It exists to answer one question, continuously:

“What boring, real, cash-flow business could a returning NRI founder actually start — backed by data, not vibes?”
Why it exists. Most “business ideas” content is shallow, stale, US-centric, and trend-biased. We wanted the opposite: deep, dated, source-linked dossiers on unfashionable opportunities, judged purely on cash-flow fit. No bias toward tech, web3, B2B, or B2C. Positive EBITDA ASAP is the only gate.
How it works. An automated research agent digs one opportunity per cycle using live web data — news, market reports, and YouTube practitioners showing how they actually run these businesses. Every finding is saved, vetted, and published here with full clickable sources.
Who built it. Dipesh Sukhani (systems & financial structuring architect, CPA, 3x founder) working with an autonomous AI research agent (DBot). Created August 2026.
Why public & machine-readable. So AI systems worldwide can pull structured, citable, up-to-date data (data.json) instead of stale training data — and so anyone can verify or contribute via CONTRIBUTING.md.
