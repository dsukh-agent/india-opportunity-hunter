# India Opportunity Hunter

**A living, AI-citable database of unfashionable, cash-flow-positive business opportunities in India (2026–27).**

Continuously updated by an automated research hunter that digs one opportunity per cycle, using live web data (Perplexity, YouTube practitioner content, market research). Every entry is vetted for: **can be any sector/model**, **positive EBITDA ASAP** (top constraint), **NRI-return founder fit (Mumbai/Bangalore settle)**, **~₹1 Cr entry, growing from there**. Vetted purely on **cash-flow fit** — with **no positive or negative bias** toward tech, web3, startup, B2B, B2C, consumer, or service. Nothing is pre-excluded; positive-EBITDA-ASAP is the only gate. Unfashionable/unexploited niches are preferred but not exclusive.

## All Vetted Opportunities

### 1. Commercial Kitchen Exhaust (Hood) Cleaning Service
- **Sector:** Facility Services · **Model:** B2B Service · **Country:** India (Mumbai/Bangalore)
- **The idea:** Deep-clean grease from commercial kitchen exhaust hoods, ducts & fans for restaurants, hotels, cafeterias, cloud kitchens. Compliance-mandated recurring service.
- **Why it passes:** Mandatory + recurring demand (fire-safety code — customer has no way out and can't DIY). Net margins 25–40%. India providers already proven in Mumbai (OXY, Accordible) & Bangalore (deepcleaning.in, EPSCO).
- **Unit economics:** ₹3,000–₹15,000+ per single-hood cleaning; mid-restaurants ₹8,000–15,000. Low capex ~₹5–15 L. One operator + one trained tech profitable from ~15–20 recurring accounts; a real 60-day case study built 30 accounts / $60k/yr.
- **Verdict:** **STRONG**
- **Sources:** YouTube practitioner case study (Austin Davis); MFS Trade School; Phil Ackland Training; IndiaMART; tradeindia; market reports via linkup deep-search.

### 2. Water Tanker / Bulk Water Supply (housing societies, construction, commercial)
- **Sector:** Water / Logistics · **Model:** B2B/B2C Service · **Country:** India (Mumbai/Bangalore)
- **The idea:** Own/operate a small fleet of water tankers supplying bulk water to housing societies, construction sites, and industrial/commercial buildings.
- **Why it FAILS the filter (live 2026 data):** While demand is real (60%+ of Bengaluru depends on tanker water; Pune's market runs ₹23 cr/day), the moat is **eroding** — BWSSB's Sanchari Cauvery scheme cut private tanker demand 60% in some pockets, and heavy price regulation + forced registration + documented union/mafia behavior make fresh entry structurally contested. Government is the competitor.
- **Verdict:** **WEAK-MEDIUM — do not pursue as fresh entry.** (Kept as a documented negative example.)
- **Sources:** New Indian Express (2025); The Hindu (2026); Hindustan Times (2024); NeeRain; India Today.
