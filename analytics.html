What counts as a visit?

Every page load is logged with its IP address, user-agent, timestamp, and referrer. We can distinguish human visitors (browsers) from AI agents / crawlers (bots like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended) by their user-agent string. That lets us show, honestly, how much real machine adoption the database is getting.

What we do with the data

Used purely to measure adoption and improve the database. No personal data is sold or shared.
