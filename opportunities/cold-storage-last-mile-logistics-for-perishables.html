4. Cold Storage Last-Mile Logistics for Perishables

STRONG

Sector: Cold Chain Logistics · Business Model: B2B Dedicated Fleet · Target Geography: Mumbai / Bangalore
Capital Required: ₹82 Lakhs – 1.13 Cr · EBITDA Margin: 14% – 18% · EBITDA Timeline: 2 – 4 Months

Business Concept & Strategy

Refrigerated small-commercial vehicles (Tata Ace EV/Reefer) providing temperature-controlled scheduled transport for dairy, meat, and pharmaceutical distributors.

🎯 5-Pillar Ground-Reality Scorecard

  • 1. Working Capital & Bad Debt (30%): 7/10 (30-day B2B contracts with dedicated monthly minimums).
  • 2. Vendor / OEM Lock-in (20%): 8/10 (Standard Tata/Ashok Leyland reefer chassis with Carrier/Thermoking cooling units).
  • 3. Labor & Route Friction (20%): 8/10 (Dedicated point-to-point delivery routes; driver + telematics temperature tracking).
  • 4. Regulatory Moat (15%): 8/10 (FSSAI cold-chain temperature integrity mandates; rising QSR and frozen food demand).
  • 5. Capital & EBITDA Velocity (15%): 8/10 (₹82L–1.13 Cr for 3-vehicle fleet; 14–18% EBITDA; breakeven in 2–4 months).

✅ Why it clears the filter (the on-ground data)

  • Exploding Frozen & Dairy Demand: Quick-commerce dark stores (Blinkit/Zepto/Instamart) and QSR chains require temperature-controlled (-18°C to +4°C) scheduled daily fulfillment.
  • Asset-Light Route Focus: Avoids the ₹15 Cr capex trap of building large cold storage warehouses; focuses entirely on high-yield dedicated reefer van routes.
  • Locked-in Monthly Capacity: Contracts are structured with guaranteed daily minimum kilometers (120–150 km/day), protecting baseline revenue.

💰 Unit Economics & P&L Breakdown

  • Fleet Capex: ₹82L – 1.13 Cr (3 refrigerated commercial vehicles, IoT temperature sensors, GPS telematics, driver onboarding, working capital buffer).
  • Revenue per Van: ₹2.9L – 3.4L/month on 26-day scheduled routes.
  • Monthly EBITDA: 14% – 18% (₹1.2L – 1.8L/month net operating profit on a 3-van fleet).
