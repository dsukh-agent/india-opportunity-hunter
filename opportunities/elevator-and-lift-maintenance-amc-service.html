8. Elevator & Lift Maintenance AMC Service

REJECTED

Sector: Facility Engineering · Business Model: B2B Service AMC · Target Geography: Mumbai / Bangalore
Capital Required: ₹60L – 1.2 Cr · EBITDA Margin: 10% – 18% · EBITDA Timeline: 18+ Months

Business Concept & Strategy

Third-party non-OEM maintenance contracts for mid-rise residential apartments & commercial spaces across Bangalore/Mumbai, competing against expensive OEM AMCs.

❌ Fatal Flaw & Rejection Reason

OEM proprietary lock-in. Modern lifts use proprietary error codes/diagnostic boards (KONE, Otis, Schindler). RWAs resist independent non-OEM maintainers due to safety liability.

❌ Fatal Flaws & Rejection Reasons

  • OEM Proprietary Lock-In: Modern elevators manufactured by KONE, Otis, Schindler, and TK Elevator lock diagnostic error codes and motherboard firmware, preventing third-party technicians from resetting faults.
  • High Scale Barrier: Requires a minimum fleet of 300+ elevators under contract before technician salary overhead is covered.
  • Safety Liability: Housing societies refuse non-OEM independent maintainers due to fear of accident liability.
