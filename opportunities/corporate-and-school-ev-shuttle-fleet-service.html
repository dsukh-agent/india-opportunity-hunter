10. Corporate & School EV Shuttle Fleet Service

WEAK

Sector: Fleet Transportation · Business Model: B2B Dedicated Route · Target Geography: Mumbai / Bangalore
Capital Required: ⏳ Under Research · EBITDA Margin: ⏳ Under Research · EBITDA Timeline: ⏳ Under Research

Business Concept & Strategy

Contracted employee pickup/drop and school route transportation using leased electric 12-seater / 24-seater vans with guaranteed annual contracts.

❌ Fatal Flaw & Rejection Reason

Fails core filters. Capital-heavy asset business: e-shuttles Rs25-45L, 9m e-buses Rs1.2-2.5Cr plus Rs5-15L/veh charging infra, so ~1Cr entry funds only 2-4 small shuttles that cannot absorb central overhead (control room/sales/depot), making corporate EBITDA negative at small scale. Not EBITDA-ASAP: operating break-even only at 10-18mo, positive corporate EBITDA only at 18-30mo and 30-50+ vehicles. School-only EV utilization too low (50-100km/day vs 120-220 corporate) per CEEW. Crowded market (Routematic, MoveInSync, Lithium Urban, Everest, WTi, Ratham, NVS, Aaveg, KTC) with thin margins.

🎯 5-Pillar Ground-Reality Scorecard

  • 1. Working Capital & Bad Debt (30%): 3/10 (60-90 day corporate receivable cycles drain cash; fuel/charging is prepaid but clients pay late; need financing buffer).
  • 2. Vendor / OEM Lock-in (20%): 5/10 (EV batteries + OEM/service network create moderate lock-in; charger/CPO dependency; but buses are commoditized across Tata/Olectra/PMI).
  • 3. Labor & Route Friction (20%): 6/10 (Driver pool + safety compliance + attendance; high turnover, must background-verify; union/permit friction in Mumbai).
  • 4. Regulatory Moat (15%): 4/10 (Permits, AIS-066 school safety, CAQM ESG-reporting tailwind helps corporates but rules do not force a specific vendor; low moat).
  • 5. Capital & EBITDA Velocity (15%): 2/10 (High capex Rs25L-2.5Cr/bus + Rs5-15L charging; corporate EBITDA negative below 30-50 vehicles; break-even 10-30mo fails EBITDA-ASAP).

✅ Why it clears the filter (the on-ground data)

  • EV fuel cost is genuinely lower: ~Rs6.6-9.5/km vs diesel ~Rs29.8/km, per UITP.
  • Corporate employee transport is a large established managed market (Routematic 300+ clients, MoveInSync 400+ companies, Lithium Urban 3,000+ EVs) with ESG reporting tailwind.
  • Vehicle-level EBITDA can reach 20-35% pre-financing at high 120-220km/day utilization on contracted routes.
