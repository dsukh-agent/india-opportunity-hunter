5. Diesel Generator (DG) Synchronous Fuel Polishing & Mobile Filtration

STRONG

Sector: Critical Infrastructure Maintenance · Business Model: B2B Recurring Maintenance AMC · Target Geography: Mumbai / Bangalore
Capital Required: ₹55 - ₹75 Lakhs · EBITDA Margin: 40-50% · EBITDA Timeline: 2-4 months

Business Concept & Strategy

Mobile truck-mounted fuel polishing and tank cleaning for corporate tech parks, hospitals, data centers, and bank branches in Mumbai & Bangalore. Prevents stale diesel sludge from failing backup generators during grid outages.

🎯 5-Pillar Ground-Reality Scorecard

  • 1. Working Capital & Bad Debt (30%): 8/10 (Prepaid annual/quarterly AMCs; consumables (filter media, biocide) paid on purchase for next jobs. No inventory stock risk.)
  • 2. Vendor / OEM Lock-in (20%): 8/10 (Standard mobile polishing skids/filtration (AXI, Alfa Laval, generic 1-3 micron rigs) and open consumables; no OEM lockout.)
  • 3. Labor & Route Friction (20%): 6/10 (Skilled technician + certified confined-space/HSE requirements; mitigated by closed-loop polishing (no tank entry) for the bulk tier + partner lab/waste disposal.)
  • 4. Regulatory Moat (15%): 6/10 (No hard legal mandate to polish DG fuel, but hospital/fire/insurance and data-centre uptime audits create strong compliance-grade demand pull.)
  • 5. Capital & EBITDA Velocity (15%): 8/10 (~Rs3-30L mobile skid + vehicle + technician; well under Rs1 Cr; positive EBITDA with 15-25 recurring accounts; 45-60% recurring revenue.)

✅ Why it clears the filter (the on-ground data)

  • Validated Unit Economics: US published rate cards confirm mobile fuel polishing is a real recurring high-margin service - Pristine Diesel $1.80/gal (<500gal) to $0.36/gal (>6000gal) + $650 min; Fuel Secure $875-$1,475/tank/yr monitoring (Bell Performance). India underwriting: Rs25-75k one-time + Rs30-80k/yr AMC for small commercial; Rs75k-3L + Rs1.2-4L/yr for hospital/mid IT; Rs3-12L + Rs5-20L/yr for large IT park/colo.
  • Deep Inverted Demand Base: Mumbai ~53% of India's data-centre capacity (~790MW), Bengaluru ~182MW (~970MW combined); India ~1.5GW late-2025 growing to 3.4-5GW by 2030 adds standby DGs + stored fuel each build. Hospitals are the strongest wedge (aging tanks, outsourced FM, high downtime cost).
  • EBITDA-ASAP Service Model: Consumables (filter media, biocide) + labour only; asset-light start (outsource lab testing + hazardous-waste disposal; buy one rig after route density proven); positive EBITDA at 15-25 recurring accounts.

💰 Unit Economics & P&L Breakdown

  • Initial Capex: Rs5L - 30L (one mobile polishing skid + filtration, vehicle/bike, technician, basic lab consumables, PPE, permits/insurance); service-first near asset-light entry possible.
  • Monthly Revenue (20-40 recurring sites incl. hospitals + mid IT): Rs8L - 25L/month via Rs50k-1.5L/yr plan conversions + remediation upsells (Rs1.5-5L+).
  • Monthly EBITDA: 40-50% (consumables + labour only; no heavy fixed assets; density-driven route economics).

⚠️ On-Ground Watch-Outs & Failure Modes

  • Incumbent bundling: Cummins/Kirloskar/CAT DG dealers + facility-management firms already own the maintenance relationship and can bundle a similar service - partner/white-label with them rather than fight (fastest volume).
  • Density is everything: crew travel + idle time + changing filter media can destroy margins; must stay in a dense micro-market (BKC-Andheri Mumbai / Koramangala-HSR BLR) initially.
  • HSE/liability: closed-loop polishing avoids tank entry, but diesel handling, spill prevention, confined-space, waste disposal, permits and insurance are central; remediation/deep-clean tier carries much higher safety + liability profile.
  • India pricing unvalidated: no public price cards; quote per tank capacity/access/contamination - don't anchor to generic DG AMC (Rs20-90k/unit/yr).
