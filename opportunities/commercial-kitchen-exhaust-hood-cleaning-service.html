1. Commercial Kitchen Exhaust (Hood) Cleaning Service

STRONG

Sector: Facility & Safety Services · Business Model: B2B Recurring Compliance · Target Geography: Mumbai / Bangalore
Capital Required: ₹5 – 15 Lakhs · EBITDA Margin: 25% – 40% · EBITDA Timeline: 1 – 3 Months

Business Concept & Strategy

Deep-clean grease from commercial kitchen exhaust hoods, ducts & fans for restaurants, hotels, cafeterias, cloud kitchens in Mumbai/Bangalore. Compliance-mandated recurring service.

🎯 5-Pillar Ground-Reality Scorecard

  • 1. Working Capital & Bad Debt (30%): 9/10 (Prepaid quarterly AMC or payment upon job completion. Zero inventory loss.)
  • 2. Vendor / OEM Lock-in (20%): 9/10 (Open commoditized degreasers, portable 3500 PSI pressure washers, no OEM lockout.)
  • 3. Labor & Route Friction (20%): 7/10 (Overnight 11pm-4am shifts; mitigated by paying 25% performance commission per hood).
  • 4. Regulatory Moat (15%): 9/10 (Mandatory NFPA-96 / local fire-safety compliance; involuntary commercial demand).
  • 5. Capital & EBITDA Velocity (15%): 10/10 (₹5–15L all-in capex; positive EBITDA from Month 1-2 with 15 accounts).

✅ Why it clears the filter (the on-ground data)

  • Involuntary Legal Demand: Commercial kitchens are legally required under fire-safety codes to degrease exhaust hoods quarterly to prevent grease fires.
  • High Gross Margin: Standard hood clean is ₹3,000–₹15,000 per kitchen; chemical and consumable cost is under ₹800/job (65–75% gross margin).
  • Fast Cash Breakeven: A 2-man crew can service 2 hoods per night (₹10,000/night revenue). 20 recurring restaurant locations generate ₹3.5–4.5L/month top-line.

💰 Unit Economics & P&L Breakdown

  • Initial Capex: ₹5.5L – 12L (Commercial hot-water pressure washer, rotary duct brushes, wet vac, scaffolding, PPE, used utility vehicle).
  • Monthly Revenue (30 restaurants, quarterly cleans): ₹4.5L – 6.0L/month including filter exchange upsells.
  • Monthly EBITDA: 28% – 42% (₹1.3L – 2.4L/month net operating cash flow).

⚠️ On-Ground Watch-Outs & Failure Modes

  • Avoid scattered geography: 40+ accounts must be within a 15km micro-market radius (e.g. Bandra-BKC-Andheri in Mumbai, or Indiranagar-Koramangala-HSR in BLR) to prevent traffic from destroying route profitability.
  • Insurance Trap: General liability policies do not cover grease fires occurring after technicians leave. Must obtain specialized completed-operations fire coverage.
