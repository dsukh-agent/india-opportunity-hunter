8. Medical Equipment Rental & Home ICU Support

REJECTED

Sector: Healthcare Logistics · Business Model: Asset Rental · Target Geography: Mumbai / Bangalore
Capital Required: ₹50L – 1.2 Cr · EBITDA Margin: 8% – 12% · EBITDA Timeline: 24 – 36 Months

Business Concept & Strategy

Rental of oxygen concentrators, hospital beds, BiPAP machines, and patient monitors to nursing homes and home-care patients in Tier-1 suburbs with monthly billing.

❌ Fatal Flaw & Rejection Reason

Asset-heavy capex with 24-36 month payback. Low utilization (<55%) kills unit economics. Nurse staffing is an operational graveyard (Portea/Emoha EBITDA negative).

❌ Fatal Flaws & Rejection Reasons

  • Asset-Heavy with Slow Payback: Buying hospital beds, oxygen concentrators, and BiPAP units requires ₹35k–₹4.5L per machine with 24–36 month payback.
  • Low Fleet Utilization: Average rental utilization hovers at 50–55%, collapsing operating margins.
  • Nurse Staffing Operational Graveyard: Bundling home-care nursing turns the model into a low-margin staffing headache (Portea and Emoha operate at negative EBITDA).
