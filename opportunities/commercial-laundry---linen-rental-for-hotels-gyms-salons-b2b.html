    📌 Specific Data Point / Regulatory Learning: India commercial laundry/linen services ~$12B/yr, highly fragmented, dominated by local providers; post-pandemic hygiene raised volumes 40%, labor costs up 15%/yr, hotel chains consolidating vendors.
  • 📄 Sanyantra — How to Start a Laundry Business in India
    📌 Specific Data Point / Regulatory Learning: A 50-100 room hotel generates 100-300 kg laundry/day; B2B needs commercial 20kg+ washer extractors, dryers, flatwork ironers, 1,000-3,000 sq ft. Labor Rs4-8k/helper/mo in Mumbai/Bangalore.
  • 📄 LaundroMentor — Laundry Business Cost & Margin
    📌 Specific Data Point / Regulatory Learning: Small-scale B2B laundry entry needs Rs8-15 lakh (machinery, infra, working capital) — fits ~1 Cr start. Rental/route economics favor bulk contracts with gyms, hostels, salons.
  • 📄 Clean India Journal — Busting Myths About Rental Linen
    📌 Specific Data Point / Regulatory Learning: Linen rental cuts a typical 100-room 4-star property running linen costs 28-30%; rental transfers capex, laundering, replacement and storage to provider; quality kept high because provider owns linen life.
  • 📄 Quick Smart Wash — Hospitality Linen Management (RFID rental)
    📌 Specific Data Point / Regulatory Learning: RFID-tracked linen rental (owns 300+ thread-count stock) locks clients into recurring monthly contracts, cuts shrinkage ~80%; fixed monthly cost; white space in Mumbai/Delhi hospitals adopting intl standards without captive plants.
  • 📄 MarkWide — Commercial Laundry Service Market
    📌 Specific Data Point / Regulatory Learning: Global commercial laundry $68.4B (2026) → $104.3B by 2035 (4.8% CAGR); linen rental fastest-growing segment; India corporate hospital chains in Mumbai/Delhi are white-space for outsourced linen.
