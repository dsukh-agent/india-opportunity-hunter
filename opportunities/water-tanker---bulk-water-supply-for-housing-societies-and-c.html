5. Water tanker / bulk water supply for housing societies & construction sites

REJECTED

Sector: Water Logistics · Business Model: Asset / Brokerage · Target Geography: Mumbai / Bangalore
Capital Required: ₹25L – 60L (Fleet) / ₹10L (Broker) · EBITDA Margin: 10% – 15% · EBITDA Timeline: 12+ Months

Business Concept & Strategy

Water tanker / bulk water supply for housing societies & construction sites

❌ Fatal Flaw & Rejection Reason

Negative pricing power. Municipal price caps (BWSSB Sanchari Cauvery), CGWA regulatory crackdown, union turf wars, and 45-60 day RWA credit stretch.

⚠️ On-Ground Watch-Outs & Failure Modes

  • Regulatory Crackdown: Central Ground Water Authority (CGWA) and BMC enforce strict commercial borewell extraction NOCs with heavy confiscation penalties.
  • Union / Mafia Friction: Local tanker syndicates aggressively block outside fleet operators from entering specific pin codes.

❌ Fatal Flaws & Rejection Reasons

  • Zero Pricing Power: BBMP/BWSSB has imposed official price caps (₹1,200 for 12,000L), making private tanker fleet ROI unviable.
  • Municipal Direct Competition: The Karnataka government's 'Sanchari Cauvery' tanker initiative deployed 150+ subsidized government tankers, cutting private demand by 60% in outer Bangalore pockets.
  • Extreme Working Capital Drag: Residential high-rises and RWAs delay tanker payments by 45–60 days while diesel and drivers require daily cash.
