2. Commercial RO & Water Purification Plant AMC

STRONG

Sector: Water Infrastructure · Business Model: B2B Service AMC · Target Geography: Mumbai / Bangalore
Capital Required: ₹12 – 25 Lakhs · EBITDA Margin: 20% – 35% · EBITDA Timeline: 3 – 6 Months

Business Concept & Strategy

Turnkey maintenance, filter replacement & water testing contracts for residential society STPs, commercial office ROs, and factory process water plants.

🎯 5-Pillar Ground-Reality Scorecard

  • 1. Working Capital & Bad Debt (30%): 9/10 (Prepaid quarterly/annual AMCs; replacement consumables paid in advance.)
  • 2. Vendor / OEM Lock-in (20%): 9/10 (Standardized membranes (Dow Filmtec/Toray) and open pumps (Grundfos/CNP).)
  • 3. Labor & Route Friction (20%): 8/10 (Daytime scheduled preventive visits; standard electrical/plumbing troubleshooting SOPs.)
  • 4. Regulatory Moat (15%): 9/10 (Mandatory PCB/CPCB STP water quality compliance for residential high-rises and factories.)
  • 5. Capital & EBITDA Velocity (15%): 9/10 (₹12–25L entry; 20–35% EBITDA margin; positive cash flow within 3 months.)

✅ Why it clears the filter (the on-ground data)

  • Non-Discretionary Infrastructure: Commercial RO plants in tech parks and society Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) cannot be turned off. When a pump or dosing system fails, emergency repair is immediate.
  • High-Margin Recurring Consumables: AMC contracts cover preventive labor, while high-ticket consumables (RO membranes, activated carbon, filter sand, dosing chemicals) are billed with 35–50% margin.
  • Prepaid Annual Contracts: RWAs and facility managers pay AMCs in quarterly advance installments.

💰 Unit Economics & P&L Breakdown

  • Entry Capex: ₹12L – 22L (Testing instruments, emergency spare parts inventory, service tools, 2 technician mobile units).
  • Typical Contract: ₹35,000 – ₹1,20,000/year per society/commercial building.
  • Target Portfolio: 35–50 active AMCs generate ₹2.8L – 4.2L/month gross profit at 25–35% net EBITDA.
