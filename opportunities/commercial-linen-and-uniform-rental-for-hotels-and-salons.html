6. Commercial Linen & Uniform Rental for Hotels & Salons

REJECTED

Sector: Commercial Laundry · Business Model: B2B Contract Rental · Target Geography: Mumbai / Bangalore
Capital Required: ₹40L – 80L · EBITDA Margin: 8% – 14% · EBITDA Timeline: 18 – 24 Months

Business Concept & Strategy

Centralized commercial laundry facility providing linen & uniform rental + daily pickup/delivery to boutique hotels, gyms, clinics, and salon chains in MMR/Bangalore on monthly retainer.

❌ Fatal Flaw & Rejection Reason

Working capital trap. 3-par linen stock capex + 15-20% annual linen shrinkage/theft + hotels stretch receivables to 60-90 days. Sub-15% EBITDA until massive scale.

❌ Fatal Flaws & Rejection Reasons

  • Working Capital Trap: Renting linen requires funding 3-par inventory upfront (1 in hotel, 1 in laundry, 1 in transit). For a 200-room hotel, linen capex exceeds ₹18–25 Lakhs.
  • 15–20% Annual Linen Theft/Shrinkage: Hotels, room service, and housekeeping staff routinely lose, tear, or stain linen, creating massive unrecoverable write-offs.
  • Severe DSO Delay: Hospitality chains delay invoice clearances to 60–90 days, suffocating lean cash flow.
